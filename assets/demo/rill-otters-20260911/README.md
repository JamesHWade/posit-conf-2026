# Otters for the Rill demo

Five new illustrations generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool on September 11, 2026. The reference is the existing Rill reading otter at `../rill-stills-20260910/rill-otter-reading.png`. Each scene was generated independently using the same character reference and shared style instructions.

The deck and gallery now use the `*-transparent.png` versions, edited with the built-in image tool on September 11, 2026 and verified to contain transparent alpha. The original warm-paper images remain alongside them. See [background-removal.md](background-removal.md) for the files, edit prompts, and checks. The Rill introduction uses the large reading otter; the other illustrations sit beside the headings so the app captures retain their reading scale.

## Prompts

### Rill

```text
Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: one standalone otter illustration for a conference presentation demo slide.
Input image 1 is a CHARACTER AND STYLE REFERENCE only, not a composition to copy. Generate a new pose of the very same Rill river otter: russet orange-brown fur, wide cream rounded muzzle and chest, glossy dark oval nose, dark rounded eyes with tiny white highlights, small round ears, curved eyebrows, long tapered tail, rounded paws with dark pads, three whiskers on each cheek. Match the reference's proportions and friendly, attentive personality. It must look like the same individual otter across a coordinated five-image set.
Style: crisp editorial storybook illustration, near-flat colors with restrained drawn fur strokes, strong dark teal ink contours, warm cream paper props and small river teal accents. Sophisticated and simple enough to read on a presentation screen. No 3D, photorealism, glossy rendering, gradients, or noisy textures.
Canvas: square 1024 by 1024. One otter, entire figure and every prop within the frame, comfortable 8 percent clear margin. Compact centered composition, character large with a clear silhouette. Background must be perfectly uniform solid warm paper color #F4F0E8 from edge to edge. No checkerboard, no simulated transparency, no border, no panel, no caption, no lettering, no watermark. Marks on pages are a few simple abstract horizontal lines, never readable words. No interface screenshots or technology logos.

Scene: A fresh version of the reading otter for the Rill introduction. The otter floats comfortably on its back at a gentle diagonal, holding an open cream article booklet in its two forepaws and looking down at the pages with quiet interest. Its hind paws and long curling tail are visible. A few compact teal water ripples cradle its body; outside these ripples is the solid paper background. Make this a new expressive pose, not an exact redraw of the reference. Keep the booklet broad and its pages clearly open. Calm pleasure in reading, no extra props.
```
